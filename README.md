# Vista 3D: Urban Decay

An open ruined city from the Vista 3D series: downtown towers, market streets with storefront signs, old suburbs, an ironworks district, a broken highway and landmarks, under a full day and night cycle. Walk or fly, and scavenge the glowing supply crates. Built with three.js (loaded from cdnjs) as a single static page.

Controls: mouse to look (click to lock, Esc to release), WASD to walk, Shift to run, G to fly (Space up, Q down). On phones: left thumb walks, right thumb looks, FLY button to take off.
